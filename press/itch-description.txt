Tron light cycles, online, in one shared arena.

Your bike leaves nothing behind — until you switch on the blade. Then it lays a wall of
light that knocks out anyone who rides into it, you included. You get about three seconds
of blade per charge and six seconds to get it back, so the whole game is picking the moment.

Everyone rides in the same arena and you can join any time. No rounds, no lobby, no waiting:
you ride, you get knocked out, you ride straight back in. Bots keep the arena at six bikes
so it is never empty, and they step aside as real riders arrive.

- Your own wall kills you too.
- Walls fade after eight seconds, and vanish the moment their owner is knocked out.
- One point a second alive, ten a knockout. The board keeps Today and All time.

Controls
- Computer: arrow keys or WASD to steer, space for the blade.
- Phone: an arrow pad and a BLADE button.

Works in any browser, on a phone or a computer. No sign-in, no ads, no install.

Made by Lok. Third in a small collection of games that run classics backwards — after
Breakin (a Breakout where you play the bricks) and Snaked (a Snake where every apple makes
you shorter).
